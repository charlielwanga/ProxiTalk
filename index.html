📡 Nearby BLE (Android)

Native BLE is available only in the Android app.

Android supports experimental one-hop, recipient-encrypted BLE text packets with ECDSA sender verification against the online directory. When online mailbox delivery fails, an enabled nearby carrier may accept a signed packet; this is only a handoff, not proof of receipt. Discovery stays on only while you enable it. Relay/store-and-forward and delivery receipts are not implemented, and physical-device testing is still required.
